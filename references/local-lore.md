# Local lore and governance (template)

Notes for "lore mode." Fill this in for your own area and extend it each
election cycle. Recurring local issues show up in nearly every local race, so
having the backstory ready saves a research pass.

## Jurisdictions on my ballot

For each body you vote for (city, county, school district, special districts):
- Legal status (incorporated city, county, independent special district, etc.)
  and what created it.
- Governing body: seats, terms, at-large or by district, partisan or not.
- Taxing authority (does it levy its own millage?) and boundaries.
- Which bodies you do NOT vote for and why (e.g. living in an unincorporated
  area outside city limits).
- Where overlapping bodies clash (shared projects, cost splits, approvals).

## Recurring local sagas

For each long-running controversy:
- One-paragraph summary.
- Timeline of key events with dates and dollar amounts.
- The factions it creates and the coded language candidates use for each side
  (e.g. "activate/develop" vs. "conserve/steward").
- Why it keeps coming back every election.
- Sources.

## Context cues
- Phrases or positions that signal which side of a local issue a candidate is
  on.
- Notes on low-salience races (e.g. unopposed incumbents who don't campaign;
  small turnout means an active challenger can win).
