# Candidate review template and output format

Give every research sub-agent the same structure and the voter's **criteria
statement** (from Step 2) so results are comparable across races and parties.

## Per-candidate research template

For EACH serious candidate on the ballot line, gather:

1. **Background:** current role, prior offices, profession, relevant biography,
   party changes. Incumbent, appointed incumbent, or challenger?
2. **Record and positions on the voter's criteria:** for each issue the voter
   named, the candidate's stated position and, for incumbents, their voting
   record or official actions. Note where a candidate breaks with their party.
   Cover the voter's issues first; add other major positions only briefly.
3. **Funding** (always, unless the voter said funding doesn't matter): total
   raised and cash on hand with the report date, self-funding and loans, major
   donors and industries, PACs and super PACs on either side, small-dollar vs.
   large-donor mix, public matching funds. Cite FEC, the state elections
   division, or the county election office.
4. **Ethics and integrity:** investigations, charges, lawsuits, conflicts of
   interest, discipline. State neutrally and cite reliable sources. Apply the
   same standard to every candidate.
5. **Endorsements:** across the spectrum, e.g. labor unions, business groups,
   chambers of commerce, issue groups on both sides of the voter's top issues,
   newspapers, police and fire unions, party figures, former officials.
6. **Viability:** frontrunner, competitive, underdog or minor; polls with
   sponsor and date; independent race ratings.

Label minor candidates clearly so an unfunded long-shot and a frontrunner
don't get equal space.

## Nonpartisan variants

- **Judicial:** replace (2) with legal background, appointing official, years of
  experience, notable opinions on the voter's issues, bar ratings or polls,
  discipline (flag if unverifiable), and reported judicial philosophy. Keep
  funding where judges campaign.
- **Special district / school board:** replace (2) with what the body does, its
  live local controversies, and each candidate's position on them.
- **Ballot measure:** plain-English effect; how it got on the ballot; threshold
  to pass; official fiscal estimate; best case for and against, each attributed
  to named supporters and opponents; money on each side; concrete effect on the
  voter where calculable.

## Per-race output format

For each race:
- **Race name**, contested or uncontested, and any rating.
- One or two tight lines per candidate: who they are; record on the voter's
  criteria; funding; ethics flags.
- A **criteria-based pick** (or "no clear pick") naming the criteria that drove
  it, with a one-line honest tradeoff.

Then a consolidated **slate** (one line per race), a **Sources** list, and a
short **couldn't verify** list.

For a saved guide, use the same structure with light headers, put the slate at
the top, and keep it readable on a phone at the polls.
